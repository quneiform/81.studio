HOW THIS SITE IS BUILT

Building this site on Quneiform.

Keeping core facts separate from themes, layout, and optional wording.

Separating Content from Presentation

Most websites mix copy, layout, and styling together in templates. When you try out a new theme or rewrite headlines for a different tone, it is easy for facts, links, or contact details to drift out of sync.

This site is packaged with Quneiform so content and presentation stay separate. Core details about our work and how to reach us live in one place, while themes, button styles, spacing, and terminal or web rendering are applied on top.

Optional Theme Wording

Each place and computing-era theme (Redmond, SGI Indigo, Puget Sound, Olympic Larch) includes optional era-flavored copy from our design explorations. Switching themes only changes colors and styling—you can turn theme wording or letter-scrambling on and off separately without affecting the underlying page data or contact address.

Selecting a visual theme never implicitly rewrites copy. Instead, visitors can toggle theme-adapted wording below or in the theme widget to preview how the engine adapts voice across eras.
